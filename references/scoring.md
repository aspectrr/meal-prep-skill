# Scoring Rubric

Score every shortlisted recipe 0-5 on each axis, then apply weights. The reheat and freezer axes are **gating** — a recipe can't win on total score alone if it fails storage.

## Axes & weights

| Axis | Weight | 5 = | 0 = |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reheat quality** | ×3 | microwave + day-5 fridge = tastes freshly made | turns to mush / dries out / separates |
| **Freezer stability** | ×2 | freezes 2-3 mo, reheats identical | inedible after freeze (soggy, mealy, split sauce) |
| **Hands-on time** | ×2 | mostly hands-off (slow cooker, sheet pan, one pot, batch-friendly) | constant attention, fiddly, multi-stage |
| **Scales to 10-12** | ×2 | multiplies cleanly, one big batch | single-egg/fish-filet/fragile technique that breaks at scale |
| **Pantry/diet fit** | ×1 | uses stuff you have, matches diet + avoids | specialty run, violates a pref |

Total max = (5×3)+(5×2)+(5×2)+(5×2)+(5×1) = **50**.

## Gating thresholds
- **Reheat < 3 → drop**, no matter the total. This is the weekly-rotation core requirement.
- **Freezer < 2 AND reheat < 4 → drop.** If it won't freeze it must at least last the fridge week.
- Diet violation or a hard-avoid ingredient → **auto-drop** (don't even score).

## Reheat red flags (score 0-2)
- Delicate greens (salads, fresh herbs as the star) — wilt to slime.
- Fried/coated things meant to be crisp — go soggy.
- Seafood cooked through then reheated — rubbery.
- Egg dishes (poached, soft) — texture dies.
- Dairy-heavy cream sauces — can split/grain on reheat.

## Reheat green flags (score 4-5)
- Braises, curries, stews, chili — flavor often *improves* next day.
- Tomato/bean/lentil bases.
- Rice + grain bowls (keep wet component separate if needed).
- Casseroles, baked pasta, lasagna.
- Slow-cooked tough cuts (pork shoulder, beef chuck, chicken thighs).

## Freezer red flags (score 0-2)
- Potatoes (get mealy/grainy) — exception: mashed freezes ok.
- Cooked pasta in soup (absorbs, turns to paste).
- High-water raw veg (cucumber, lettuce, watermelon).
- Mayonnaise/egg emulsions — break.
- Delicate fish.

## Freezer green flags (score 4-5)
- Soups, stews, chili, braises (strain pasta/rice out first).
- Sauces, curries, ragù.
- Cooked beans, lentils.
- Shredded meats (pulled pork/chicken).
- Burritos, enchiladas, empanadas, dumplings.
- Quick breads, muffins, cookie dough.

## Hands-on scoring guide
- **5**: dump + walk away (slow cooker, Instant Pot, oven braise) or prep-once-use-all-week.
- **3**: one pan, ~20 min active then oven/unattended.
- **1**: multi-component, plating, last-minute sear/fry per serving.

## Scale scoring guide
- **5**: liquid-based or bulk (soups, curries, chili, shredded meat) — just use a bigger pot.
- **3**: casseroles/pasta — need a bigger dish, maybe +20% bake time.
- **1**: pan-fried individual portions, delicate proteins, anything needing exact ratios (some baking).

## Worked example
**Thai massaman curry (beef), scaled 4×**
- Reheat 5 ×3 = 15 (curry improves next day)
- Freezer 5 ×2 = 10
- Hands-on 4 ×2 = 8 (chop + simmer unattended)
- Scale 5 ×2 = 10 (just bigger pot + more coconut milk)
- Pantry 4 ×1 = 4
- **Total 47/50** — strong pick.
